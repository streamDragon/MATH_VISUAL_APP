# Vizy critique and improvement plan (23 Nov 2026)

Full version with the tools table: Haggai's Claude doc "Vizy — critique and improvement plan (23 Nov 2026)". This is the short form for agents.

## Verdict

The lessons are careful and correct; the app around them does not yet feel alive, visual or like one product.

1. It does not show its promise. The first grade 9 screen is a symbol choice with no picture ("see before solving" is missing at the door).
2. The mascot is a still image: no reaction, no celebration.
3. It looks like several apps: dark home, white lesson cards, other headers and buttons in guided practice, two navigation systems on the default problem map. On a computer it was a phone strip in the middle (fixed 23 Nov).

## Keep

One question per screen; tap answers, no typing in inquiry; the general ↔ particular map; formulas left to right; hints that ask; the concept lens activity (picture first, evidence by touch); the green test suite.

## Screen notes (beyond FLOW_REVIEW_2026-11-21.md)

- Home: the grade switch sits in the hero under "הפעילות הבאה"; the title "רעיון אחד שמגלגלים" says nothing. Ask the grade once; hero = picture of today's idea + one sentence.
- Journey: circles 1–4 have no names; "זמן משחק 0:00" on day one. Name the stops; hide the clock until earned.
- Lesson after the opening answer: the page opens scrolled down (map top cut off) and repeats the same question with the same answers. Start at the top with the answer marked.
- Celebration: the spark burst in `vizy-sound.js` only has its `#celebration` holder on `learn.html`; inquiry lessons get no visual burst.
- Guided practice: other header, blue main button, typed `#answerInput`.
- "כלים ועוד" drawer: nine links; keep it to four student tools.
- Android: the AdMob plug-in is in the build (debug uses Google's test ID). Decide before any store release (Google Play Families rules).

## Plan, in order

1. Living mascot (CSS): `vizy-mascot.js/.css`, `VizyMascot.react(state)` driven by `vizy:progress`.
2. Fireworks on discoveries: self-hosted canvas-confetti from the dragged point or chosen chip; never on a wrong answer.
3. Home says what Vizy is: grade once, picture hero, named journey stops, clock hidden until earned.
4. One look: dark lesson cards, white only inside pictures, one header and one navigation, shared buttons.
5. Picture-first opening for difference of squares: square minus square, drag B, the L-shape folds into (A−B)(A+B).
6. No typing in guided practice (`practice-page-agent-instructions-4.md`).
7. Rive character behind the same `react(state)` API.

The six lesson fixes in FLOW_REVIEW_2026-11-21.md fit between steps 4 and 5.

## Haggai's decisions

Mascot character (keep the lime square or a new one); Rive designer or remix or CSS only; ads on Android; a full two-column computer layout or not; still open: sound default, play time and game, algebra grade.
